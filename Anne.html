Anne Frank

Anne Frank is a very young, Jewish girl that lives in Amesterdam. She lived in Amesterdam during WW2. Since she was a Jew at some point she did have to go into hiding. That day came on July 6, 1942. The reason to go into hiding was because Margot, Anne's sister, recieved a call-up notice which is saying you have to go to the labor camps. Of course her parents didn't want that happening, so they went into hiding. She got caught 2 years later on August 4th, 1944. People think somebody that knew that the family was in hiding and reported it to the athorities to save their own family, or an anonymous informant.

Anne Franks's Diary


Anne was given the diary on her 13th birthday on June 12, 1942. She kept writing in it until her last entry, on August 1st, 1944. She talked about everything, from politics, to body parts. She wrote all the time about her crush, Peter. Not all entries where published, and some names did have to be censored, but in the end, it was 368 pages long! It took me around 2-3 weeks of reading at night to finally finish the book. The ending was anticlimactic because how was she supposed to know that she would be caught just 3 days later. Anne did have a few close calls, but was finally caught. The last sentence was:"I only wish I could see some results or, just once, receive encouragement from someone who loves me."

Why her diary is Important

To a readers POV you might think that it's just another book that you have to read for school or for a tutor, but it's way more than that. To people reaserching WWII, it is definently very needed, I mean it gives a POV of someone in the war, hiding as a jew. That's everything they could have asked for. But for you, a normal reader, it is really important too. It teaches lots of things. You can learn a lot of things just because of the fact that her family discusses it. Now the only downside,(I kind-of have to say, incase you wouldn't want to read anything about it) is the fact that's it's not only about politics and family things but also stuff about sex( like body parts and puberty). I'm not saying that you still shouldn't read it. It's a really good book. Somethings might not be for somebody's age group, so you can just skip it if needed.
