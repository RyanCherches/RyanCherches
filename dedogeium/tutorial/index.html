Home Base

The home page is your hub. From there you can jump into Customize, Adventure, Arena, Updates, promo codes, and daily rewards.

Claim daily rewards whenever they are ready. Streaks give bigger currency drops and sometimes boost charges too.

Adventure Progress

Adventure mode is your main progression path. Clear each world to unlock the next one and earn currency for upgrades.

This tutorial now unlocks Level 1, so the normal campaign starts after you finish this page.

Battle Flow

Levels open with dialogue, then your doge and the enemy trade attacks until one side drops. Wins move progression forward.

Level victories also pay currency, and some levels open a victory shop so you can buy new doges right away.

Customize

Your inventory lives in Customize. Doges have rarities, and stronger rarities give better damage and health bonuses.

Equip the best doges you have to raise your stats before tougher levels or arena fights.

Shop, Selling, Exchange

Currency buys new doges, extra equip slots, and boosts. You can also sell doges you do not want.

The exchange system lets you trade several lower-rarity doges for a stronger one, so duplicates still matter.

Boosts

Boosts are bought or earned as charges. They do not start automatically, so open Customize and turn them on when you want them.

Currency Boost increases reward income, and Luck Boost helps push drops toward better rarities.

Arena

Arena is multiplayer combat. Your equipped doges define your arena stats, so gearing up in Customize helps here too.

You can fight nearby players on the same server link and earn extra rewards from wins.

Settings and Extras

Settings lets you control music volume, skip level dialogue, and turn spoken dialogue voices on or off.

Promo codes and seasonal stuff like April Fools mode can also change what shows up in your save.
