What is Space?

Space is everywhere. You are in space right now! The reason for the study of space,astronomy, is the fact that sadly the earth will come to an end. This date is around 1.3 billion years. When the earth will come to the end, humanity will end. We don't want that to happen, so astronomy exists to study different places and find earth like/habitable planets. Humans can also colonize planets, like mars, which is still pretty close to earth but can be the start of space travel to inhabit even more planets/moons.

Why Should we Learn About Space

I already said it a bit in the introduction. To say it again, the reason we need to learn about space, is because in a while(But at some point), the earth will end, and anything on it. It also helps with internet, which a lot of people both need and want. There are probably way more reasons but those are just a few.

How Cool it Really is

There are lots of reasons for astronomy/space being cool. There are lots of anomalies in space like the star called betelgeuse, which has a very irregular shape. Also the fact that it is so vast, which can mean lots of cool discoveries. As I said multiple times, this will be the future. Finally, it does have good pay, so that you can be financially off well.
